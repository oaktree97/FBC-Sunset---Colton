Why We Give

We give because God first gave to us. Your generosity helps us worship together, serve our community, support missions, and invest in the next generation through Sunset Kids and Sunset Students.

"Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver."

— 2 Corinthians 9:7
